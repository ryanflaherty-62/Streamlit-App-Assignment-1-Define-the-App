## Project Description
The Real Estate Investment Analyzer is a Streamlit app that helps users evaluate potential rental property deals in a simple and organized way. Users can enter basic property and financial assumptions, and the app calculates key investment metrics that update instantly based on those inputs. The app is designed to make real estate analysis easier to understand for students, beginner investors, and anyone learning how investment properties are evaluated.

## Target Users
This app is intended for students, beginner investors, and individuals interested in understanding real estate investment performance. It is especially useful for those who are new to real estate or finance and want a straightforward way to evaluate potential investments without needing advanced technical skills or financial modeling experience.

## Final Features
- Streamlit app title and project description
- Input widgets for purchase price, down payment, monthly rent, monthly operating expenses, mortgage interest rate, vacancy rate, closing costs, and investment goal
- Dynamic calculation of gross annual rent and effective annual rent after vacancy
- Dynamic calculation of estimated mortgage payment, monthly cash flow, net operating income, cap rate, and cash-on-cash return
- A recommendation message and decision label that change based on the user’s inputs
- Color-coded cash flow output to make positive and negative results easier to read
- A simple bar chart comparing annual rent, expenses, debt service, and cash flow

## How To Use The App
1. Enter the purchase price, down payment, monthly rent, and monthly operating expenses.
2. Adjust the mortgage interest rate, vacancy rate, and closing costs to match your property assumptions.
3. Choose your primary investment goal from the dropdown menu.
4. Review the updated outputs, including annual rent values, mortgage payment, monthly cash flow, NOI, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, recommendation label, and chart.
